Executive Council

The elected lay leaders who serve our community — organising our worship, our gatherings and our care for one another.

Who Serves

Leading in service

Our Executive Council is drawn from the members of the community and works alongside the clergy of Blessed Maria Gabriella Parish. They give their time freely so that our Sunday Mass, our ministries and our celebrations can flourish.
