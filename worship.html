The Sacraments

Also available at the parish

Blessed Maria Gabriella Parish offers the full sacramental life of the Church. Speak with a member of our community or the parish office to arrange any of the following.

Baptism

Welcoming new members of the Body of Christ, with preparation classes offered through the parish.

First Communion & Confirmation

Faith formation to prepare children and adults to receive the Eucharist and the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

Marriage & Anointing

Sacraments celebrating and strengthening the family, arranged with the parish office in advance.
