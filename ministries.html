Ministries & Groups

There is a place for every gift here. Come grow in faith and friendship with fellow Ghanaian Catholics.

01

Ghanaian Catholic Charismatic Renewal

A prayer group devoted to praise, worship and the charismatic gifts of the Holy Spirit, meeting regularly at Blessed Maria Gabriella Parish. Part of the wider Ghanaian Catholic Charismatic Renewal — North America (GCCRNA) network of prayer groups across the continent.

02

Choir & Music Ministry

Our choir leads the congregation in Ghanaian hymns, traditional drumming and joyful praise, bringing the sound of home into every Mass.

03

Hospitality & Welcoming Committee

Greeting newcomers, ushering, and organizing fellowship after Mass so that every visitor feels the warmth of Ghanaian hospitality — Akwaaba.

04

Women's & Men's Fellowship

Groups for the women and men of the community to grow together in prayer, mutual support, and service to the parish and wider community.

05

Youth & Young Adults

Nurturing the next generation of Ghanaian Catholics — passing on the faith, language and culture through fellowship, mentorship and service.

06

Cultural & Community Events

Celebrating feast days, Ghana's national holidays, and community milestones together — food, dance, and fellowship included.

Get involved

Whether you have a season, a Sunday, or a lifetime to give — there is a ministry for you. Reach out to the parish office and we'll connect you with a group leader.
